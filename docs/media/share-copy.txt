LINKEDIN / LONG POST
--------------------
Amazon is loud. "Only 3 left!", sponsored results, EMI upsells, and a ₹40 delivery fee in small print.

So I built a browser extension that makes it calm and honest:
• Shows extra fees next to the price, and makes cart delivery charges hard to miss
• Price per 100 g / 100 ml in search, so pack sizes compare fairly
• Hides sponsored results, urgency text and recommendation rails
• Auto-applies coupons, adds Keepa / price-history and reviews buttons
• Tells you which cart items a cashback link actually tracks

Version 5.0.0 now runs on Chrome, Edge and Firefox. 24 settings, grouped by the part of Amazon they change. No data leaves your browser; the only permission is storage.

As a QA engineer, I shipped it with 11 automated Playwright tests — which caught a real re-render loop and a unit-price bug from the old version.
