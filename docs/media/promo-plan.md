# Brag plan: Amazon — My Personal UX v5.0.0

## What it is
A browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) that makes Amazon quiet and honest.

## Who it is for
People who shop on amazon.in or amazon.com and are tired of clutter, fake urgency and fees in small print.

## What sets it apart
It does not only hide things. It adds facts that Amazon hides: the real delivery fee next to the price,
the price per 100 g, who the seller is, and which cart items will earn cashback.

## Most impressive claim
"+₹40 delivery fee" next to the price, before you click Buy.

## Visual hook
A loud Amazon page with three red markers (fake urgency, EMI upsell, fee in small print), then one click
and the page wipes clean.

## Real UI used
The real extension (content scripts + content.css + popup) running in Chromium on local copies of
Amazon pages. All demo data uses the name Mousy. Nothing comes from the live site.

## Tone
`default`: punchy, clean, soft transitions. Background: Sandeep's teal-green gradient.

## Share caption
Amazon, minus the noise. My Personal UX 5.0 hides the clutter, shows the fees, and tells you which cart items earn cashback.
